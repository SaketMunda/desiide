# Connect a model

Two ways in, and you can mix them:

- **Local, free, private.** If [Ollama](https://ollama.com) is running, Desiide finds it. One click makes a local model your *cheap* model. Nothing leaves your machine.
- **Cloud.** Pick Anthropic, OpenAI, OpenRouter or Gemini, paste a key, and hit **Test**. Keys go to your system keychain, never to `settings.json`.

Run **Desiide: Set Up Models** any time to come back here.
