# Desiide Midnight and Desiide Daylight

Desiide opens in **Midnight**: calm, low-glare surfaces for late sessions, lime for the one thing you're about to do, violet keywords, cyan functions, amber strings. **Daylight** is its light twin.

Any VS Code theme works too. Desiide's panels follow whichever one you pick and keep their state colors: lime done, violet decision, cyan running, amber needs you, red blocked.
