# Code of conduct

Desiide follows the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).

In short: be kind, assume good faith, and critique the code, not the person. No harassment, insults, or unwelcome attention, in any project space: issues, pull requests, discussions, or our social channels.

To report a problem, message the maintainer privately on X ([@desiideonx](https://x.com/desiideonx)) or open a private report from the repo's **Security** tab. Reports are kept confidential. Maintainers may remove comments, close threads, or block people who break these rules.
