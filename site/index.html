How it works

An agent you can actually trust with your repo.

Bring your own models

Your models. Your keys. Your machine.

Run a local model with Ollama, or plug in your own Anthropic or OpenAI-compatible key. Desiide tries cheap and local first, and only reaches for a stronger model when the task needs it. No Desiide account, no Desiide cloud.

settings · models 
❯ desiide models
  id              where   role     cost
  qwen3.5:2b      local   cheap    $0.00
  claude-opus-5-5 cloud   strong   your key
  gpt-oss-20b     local   reviewer $0.00
  keys live in your OS keychain · never in files or logs
Inspect every decision

Every call it makes, on the record.

Which model, which workflow, which risk call, and why. Each decision is logged with its reasons and odds, and you can replay it later. The decision layer sees file names and sizes, never your code.

Review before it lands

Nothing lands without your yes.

Edits arrive as diffs. Commands with side effects ask first, and anything on the hard deny-list never runs at all. Approve, reject, or undo with ⌘Z, like any other edit.
